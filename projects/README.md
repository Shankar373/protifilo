# Client-Ready Project Concepts

These are **portfolio/demo concepts**, not claimed client work. They are designed to demonstrate the kinds of problems Shankar can solve for real businesses.

## 1. Local Business Lead Website
**Target:** clinics, consultants, home services, agencies, local shops

A fast responsive website with services, trust sections, enquiry form, WhatsApp CTA, Google Maps and basic SEO structure.

**Demonstrates:** responsive UI, forms, conversion-focused UX, deployment.

## 2. Service Booking Dashboard
**Target:** salons, trainers, repair businesses, consultants

Customers can view services, choose an available slot and submit a booking. Owners get a simple dashboard to manage appointments and statuses.

**Demonstrates:** authentication, CRUD, REST APIs, database design, dashboards.

## 3. Small Business CRM
**Target:** small sales/service teams

A lightweight CRM for leads, follow-ups, notes and pipeline stages with search, filters and summary metrics.

**Demonstrates:** full-stack application design, role-based access, database queries and practical business workflows.

## 4. E-commerce Starter Store
**Target:** small product businesses

Product catalogue, search/filtering, product details, cart, checkout-ready flow and an admin area for product management.

**Demonstrates:** e-commerce UX, state management, APIs, database integration and responsive design.

## 5. Business Analytics Dashboard
**Target:** small businesses that track sales/leads/orders

A clean dashboard showing KPIs, trends, tables, filters and export-ready reports from a sample business dataset.

**Demonstrates:** data visualization, dashboard UX, API integration and business-oriented presentation.

## 6. AI FAQ / Support Assistant
**Target:** businesses with repetitive customer questions

A website assistant that answers questions from an approved knowledge base and provides a clear fallback to human contact.

**Demonstrates:** AI integration, backend APIs, prompt/knowledge handling and practical UX.

## Recommended build order

1. Local Business Lead Website
2. Service Booking Dashboard
3. Small Business CRM
4. E-commerce Starter Store
5. Analytics Dashboard
6. AI Support Assistant

Build the first three to a polished, deployable standard before adding more. The goal is not to collect many projects; it is to show credible examples of the work clients are likely to hire you for.
